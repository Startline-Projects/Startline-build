The Brief

Masjids broadcasting Athan over WhatsApp voice notes.

The client runs a network of Masjids and wanted to replace fragmented, manual community Athan notifications. Members were getting prayer time reminders from WhatsApp forwards, inconsistent broadcasts, and missed announcements. They needed a live broadcasting platform — not another notification app.

The Scope

Live broadcast. Verified Muadhin. Smart TV mode.

Three distinct experiences in one product. The listener app for community members. The verified Muadhin broadcast interface — authenticated, live, with waveform animation. And a Smart TV digital signage mode for Masjid screens showing prayer schedule and live broadcast status.

What We Built

Three experiences. One platform.

📡
Live Athan Broadcasting
Real-time audio broadcast from verified Muadhin directly to listeners. No delay. No recording. Live only.
📍
GPS Masjid Discovery
App detects nearby Masjids using location. Users connect to their nearest Masjid with one tap.
✅
Verified Muadhin System
Only verified Muadhins can broadcast. Authentication and live status prevents unauthorized broadcasts.
📺
Smart TV Signage Mode
Full-screen Masjid digital signage. Prayer schedule, live broadcast indicator, and waveform animation.
🕌
Prayer Time Display
Accurate prayer times for the user's location with countdown to next prayer. Configurable per Masjid.
🎙
Live Waveform Animation
Real-time audio waveform during live broadcasts. Listeners know the broadcast is live, not a recording.
