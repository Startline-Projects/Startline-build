Medical Scribe for Nurses — AI-Powered Clinical Documentation for Home Health

89 API endpoints • 4 user roles • HIPAA compliant • Real-time AI • Field-level encryption

The Brief

Nurses spending more time documenting than with patients.

Home health nurses were spending 2–3 hours per shift on clinical documentation — writing SOAP notes, charting vitals, documenting wound care — on paper or in clunky EHR systems not built for mobile. The client needed a voice-first AI platform that reduced documentation time by 70% while maintaining full HIPAA compliance.

The Scope

Voice in. Clinical note out. QA reviewed. Submitted.

A full clinical documentation workflow — nurse starts a visit, speaks into the app, AI transcribes and structures the note, supervisor reviews in the QA queue, and the note is submitted to the EHR. Four roles: nurse, supervisor, admin, and patient. 89 API endpoints. Real-time AI. Full audit trail for compliance.

What We Built

Every step of the clinical workflow. End to end.
