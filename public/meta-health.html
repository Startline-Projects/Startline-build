The Problem

Home health runs on paper.
That costs everyone.

2.5h
Lost every day to documentation
The average home health nurse spends 2.5 hours charting after patient visits — from memory, after an 8-hour day. Details get lost. Notes get incomplete. Claims get denied.
23%
Of claims rejected for bad notes
Incomplete or vague clinical documentation is the number one reason home health claims get rejected. Every rejection delays reimbursement by weeks and triggers expensive manual rework by QA staff.
6d
Average billing delay from QA backlog
QA nurses manually review every note before billing can start. Notes go back to the field. The billing cycle stalls. Cash flow suffers. And the nurses who wrote the notes have already moved on to the next visit.
The Solution Architecture

One platform. Three command levels.

Meta-Health is built as a true multi-tenant SaaS with three distinct user tiers — each with their own role, permissions, and purpose-built experience.

🏛️
Super Admin
Manages the entire platform. Onboards agencies. Controls billing, licensing, and platform-wide settings.
🏢
Agency Admin
Runs a specific home health agency. Manages their nurses, QA queue, reports, and billing dashboard.
🏥
Nurse
Field clinician. Records visits, receives real-time AI charting suggestions, and signs notes before leaving the patient's home.
01 · Super Admin

The command center that runs the whole platform.

The Super Admin has God-mode access. They onboard new home health agencies, assign license tiers, monitor platform-wide revenue, and see every nurse, every note, and every QA decision across all agencies in one dashboard.

✦
One-tap agency onboarding — name, admin email, license tier, Stripe billing — all wired in one form
✦
Live platform revenue dashboard — MRR, ARR, and per-plan breakdowns updated in real time
✦
Full agency drill-down — click any agency to see every nurse, note, QA status, and visit log
✦
Three license tiers — Starter (1–25 nurses), Growth (26–100), Enterprise (100+) with automatic seat enforcement

02 · Agency Admin

One Excel file. Every nurse onboarded in seconds.

The Agency Admin manages their home health operation without needing any technical knowledge. They upload a nurse roster spreadsheet — one file with all nurse emails — and the platform handles invitations, account creation, and permission assignment automatically.

✦
Excel import — upload your .xlsx nurse roster, we parse every email and send invitations automatically
✦
Live roster management — see who's active, who hasn't accepted their invite, and deactivate access with one tap
✦
QA queue ownership — review returned notes, approve completed ones, and track QA pass rates per nurse
✦
Agency-level reporting — visit completion, billing readiness, and nurse performance — all in one dashboard
03 · Nurse

Voice-first documentation. Finish the note before you leave.

The nurse opens the app, taps Start Visit, and records the patient encounter by talking — exactly as they already do. Noora, the AI, listens in real time, extracts vitals and clinical findings, and presents suggestions the nurse accepts or rejects with one tap. The chart is done before they leave the house.

✦
10-day rolling schedule — all visits in one view, GPS check-in on arrival, reschedule requests in-app
✦
Noora AI — real-time voice transcription extracts BP, weight, symptoms, and medications automatically
✦
Clinical alerts — hard stops for critical vitals, physician notification triggers, and SOB + BP correlation flags
✦
Sign and submit on-site — digital signature, instant QA submission, and confirmation before leaving the patient
