The Brief

A barber with no control over his own time or income.

The client was managing bookings over DM, getting no-shows with zero consequences, and watching his best clients miss slots because someone else booked first. After-hours demand existed but had no way to be monetized. He needed a system that locked in his best clients and charged premium rates for premium availability — automatically.

The Scope

Two apps. One ecosystem. Guaranteed income floor.

A dark-themed barber management app and a clean light-themed client booking app — distinct UX systems, shared backend, fully integrated payments. The barber controls his services, pricing tiers, and recurring slots. The client books, locks in a weekly slot, and auto-charges before every appointment. No-shows are structurally eliminated.

What We Built

Two apps. Six features. Zero no-shows.

↻
Recurring Slot System
Barbers offer weekly or biweekly locked slots. Auto-charge fires 24 hours before each appointment. Pause up to 4 weeks per year.
Lock-in model
💰
Three Booking Tiers
Regular, after-hours premium, and day-off slots each carry different prices — set by the barber, enforced by the app. Demand priced automatically.
Dynamic pricing
⚡
Auto-Charge on Recurring
Card on file auto-charges before each appointment. Failed payments surface with a 31-hour resolution window before the slot releases.
Stripe Connect
📊
Income Analytics
Standard vs recurring revenue split. Weekly, monthly, and yearly views. Recurring projected monthly shown in real time as slots are added.
Business intelligence
📱
Dual UX Systems
The barber app is dark — gold on charcoal, density-optimized. The client app is warm cream — comfortable, browseable. Two audiences. Two design languages.
React Native
💬
In-App Messaging
Direct messaging between barber and clients. Full conversation history. No personal numbers exchanged. Everything stays inside the platform.
Real-time
