The Brief

One school. Nine separate systems. All broken.

The client was running a private Islamic school with student records in spreadsheets, fees tracked manually, and parent communication over WhatsApp. Staff were spending hours every week on administration that should take minutes. They needed a single platform their entire school could run on — from the principal's dashboard to the parent's phone.

The Scope

A full school OS. Nine modules. One login.

We scoped and built a complete school administration and learning management system. Four user roles — admin, teacher, parent, and student — each with their own dashboard and permissions. Every module connects. Data flows between them automatically. No integrations, no third-party tools, no workarounds.

What We Built

Nine modules. Every one connected.

🏫
Admin Dashboard
School-wide overview. Enrollment counts, attendance rates, fee collection, and alerts — all in one view.
📋
Enrollment
Online application flow. Document collection, approval workflow, and waitlist management.
👤
Student Records
Full student profiles. Academic history, attendance record, parent contacts, and documents in one place.
📊
Gradebook
Assignment creation, grading, and automated report card generation. Teachers grade once — parents see instantly.
✅
Attendance
Daily attendance with one click per student. Automatic parent notification on absence. Monthly reports auto-generated.
👨‍👩‍👧
Parent Portal
Parents see grades, attendance, announcements, and fee status in real time. No more chasing teachers over WhatsApp.
💳
Fees & Billing
Tuition schedules, payment tracking, receipts, and overdue alerts. Parents pay online. Finance sees everything.
🤖
AI Assistant
Built-in AI that answers staff questions, generates report summaries, and drafts parent communications.
📅
Teacher Dashboard
Teacher's daily view — their classes, today's attendance, pending grades, and upcoming deadlines.
