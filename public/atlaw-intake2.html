§ VI · Section Six of Six

Legal & Compliance.

Privacy policy, terms of service, attorney advertising rules, accessibility. The compliance layer of the website.

No. 21Required

Existing privacy policy and terms of service.

If they exist, we audit and update for the new site. If they don't, we draft Michigan-bar-compliant versions for Dewnya's review.

No. 22Required

Preferred attorney advertising disclaimer language.

Michigan bar rules require attorney advertising disclaimers on the firm's website. The disclaimer goes in the footer of every page and at the bottom of any results-related content.

No. 23Required

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) commitment.

Law firm websites are an active target for ADA litigation. WCAG 2.1 AA compliance is the legal-industry standard and substantially reduces exposure. Strongly recommend yes.

No. 24Optional

Live chat or AI chatbot on the site.

Many firms get value from live chat for after-hours intake; many lose value from a low-quality chatbot that frustrates visitors. We'll discuss specifics on the kickoff if you want one.
