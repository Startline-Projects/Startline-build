§ V · Section Five of Six

Intake & Conversion.

What turns a visitor into a client. The questions only Dewnya can answer about who the firm wants to hear from and how fast someone responds when they reach out.

No. 20Required

For each Lead practice, what's the minimum threshold a matter has to clear for the firm to want a phone call about it?

Determines whether the website intake form pre-qualifies matters or accepts everything. Bad-fit leads drain attorney time more than they help.

No. 21Required

What 3 to 5 questions does the firm ask every prospective client before deciding whether to take the call?

Built into the website intake form so the questions get asked once, upfront, in writing. Instead of three times on three different phone calls.

No. 22Required

When a lead comes in, who responds first, and how fast?

Sets the autoresponder copy and the visible response-time promise on the contact page. A lawyer's site that says "we'll respond within 24 hours" while a competitor says "within an hour" loses the lead.

0 / 600
No. 23Required

How should the website handle fee information?

"Free PI consultation," "flat fee for wills starting at $X," "hourly rate for business work," or "all matters discussed by consultation"?

§ VI · Section Six of Six

Project Logistics.

The cadence of the work. The contact who decides. The deadline if there is one. The last few questions before the brief is complete.

No. 24Required

Who is the single point of contact at ATLAW for this project?

If the answer is "me," fine. If it's an executive assistant or operations lead, name them. Without a single decision-maker, the project bogs down.

No. 25Required

How often do you want to see progress?

Defines the operating rhythm. Pick the cadence you'll actually keep, not the one that sounds best.

No. 26Required

When does the new site need to be live?

A speaking engagement, a press moment, an event, a fiscal year, or a soft target. Hard deadlines get planned around; soft targets get tracked but don't drive sequencing.

No. 27Optional · The last word

What do you want this website to do that no website you've ever seen has done?

Open-ended. The one thing that, if we get it right, would make this project feel like a win. Sometimes the best answers come last.
